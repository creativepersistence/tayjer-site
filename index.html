The story

About TayJer

TayJer — Jeremy Taylor Ayala-Oquendo — is a hip-hop artist born and raised on the outskirts of Erie, Pennsylvania. He was writing and rapping before he ever set foot in high school, and he has been building his catalog ever since.

His sound moves between raw, technical lyricism and melodic hooks, shaped by the golden era as much as the new school. Along the way he has traded verses with national names — Bizzy Bone on GitBy, Stevie Stone on Dusk — and turned Erie’s own streets and landmarks into the backdrop for music videos that put his city on the map.
